## Regarding the project

Welcome to Lucky Jones, the candy store in Rotterdam where you can explore an extensive range of American candy products. With us, you'll find a variety of delicious treats that tantalize the taste buds of many Dutch people and give them the opportunity to experience American candy culture.

Our project at Lucky Jones was unique and set apart from other projects. We faced the challenging task of connecting the website inventory to the cash register system, dealing with an API that deviates from the standard protocols we are accustomed to. This required precise coordination and integration between the two systems to ensure smooth and efficient order processing.

Our experts worked with dedication and technical skills to implement the necessary solutions. We ensured seamless communication between the cash register system and the website, ensuring that the inventory is always up to date and customers can have a hassle-free shopping experience.

At Lucky Jones, we take pride in our ability to tackle complex challenges and provide innovative solutions. Our goal is to ensure customer satisfaction and create a unique shopping experience where everyone can enjoy the delicious flavors from America.
