In this research I will analyze the various tools that are found in the literature research, see _NFI3 – Desk Research.pdf_. I have categorized this document in various chapters.

- **Chapter 4 and 5** focus on the various tools that were found, pertaining to acquiring a memory dump and how they operate and function.
- In **chapter 6** we will focus on problems pertaining to the kernel and how to acquire a memory dump, by bypassing the `kernel_lockdown`.
- In **chapter 7** we will look at the various tools pertaining to analyzing the memory dump.
- From **chapter 8 onwards** we will look at the results of the forensic quality of these tools and which tool is fitting the ISO standard and the `requirementsanalyse`.
- The **results in chapter 10** are based on the research from chapter 4 till chapter 7. The results in the forensic quality research are also based on literature research.
